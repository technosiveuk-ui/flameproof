FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our fire protection services and AMC packages.

What is a Fire System Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)?
A Fire System AMC is a comprehensive yearly service contract that includes regular inspection, testing, and maintenance of all fire protection systems (fire alarms, pumps, hydrants, detectors) to ensure they remain fully operational and compliant with safety regulations.
How often should fire protection systems be serviced?
Fire protection systems should be serviced at least quarterly, with a comprehensive annual inspection. Our AMC packages include scheduled preventive maintenance visits, emergency support, and compliance documentation to keep your systems inspection-ready year-round.
Do you offer 24/7 emergency breakdown support?
Yes. Flame Proof provides round-the-clock emergency breakdown support. Our certified technicians are available 24/7 to respond to critical fire system failures, ensuring your property remains protected at all times.
What types of properties do you serve?
We serve commercial buildings, industrial facilities, residential complexes, hospitals, schools, hotels, warehouses, and retail establishments. Our solutions are customized to meet the specific fire safety requirements of each property type.
Are your technicians certified?
Yes. All Flame Proof technicians are fully certified and trained in the latest fire protection technologies. We comply with national and international fire safety standards including NFPA, BIS, and local fire department regulations.
